DocuScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

DocuScan is a document scanner and PDF utility designed around local processing. This policy explains what the app accesses, stores, shares, and lets you delete.

1. Data processing

Core document scanning, image enhancement, PDF operations, and bundled OCR are designed to run on your Android device. The release app is intended not to send scanned document contents or extracted OCR text to a developer-operated server.

2. Camera and files

Camera permission is requested only when you choose to scan with the camera. You can also select images and PDFs through Android system pickers. DocuScan does not require broad "all files" storage access for its core workflow.

3. Local data stored by the app

Depending on the features you use, DocuScan may store locally:

  • PDF documents created or imported into app-managed storage;
  • document thumbnails;
  • document metadata such as names, page counts, subjects, and timestamps;
  • OCR text used for persistent on-device search;
  • Study Mode subjects and preferences;
  • temporary cache and PDF-tool output files; and
  • limited local technical crash reports used for troubleshooting.

4. Accounts

DocuScan core features do not require a DocuScan account or sign-in.

5. Sharing and public copies

Nothing is shared merely because you scan a document. If you explicitly choose Android Share, the file or text is provided to the app you select. That recipient app may create its own copy, which DocuScan cannot control or delete afterward.

If you explicitly choose a public Documents or Downloads save location on supported Android versions, DocuScan may create a user-visible copy outside app-private storage. New public copies created by versions that support export tracking can be recorded so the app can offer deletion of known copies. Older, manually copied, moved, or recipient-created files may remain outside DocuScan's control.

6. Android backup

DocuScan disables Android application backup for its app-managed files, database, and preferences in the release configuration.

7. Optional Google Play purchase

DocuScan may offer an optional non-consumable Lifetime Pro purchase for advanced PDF editing tools. Google Play handles the payment transaction. DocuScan does not receive your card number or payment credentials.

The app can receive product, purchase-state, and purchase-token information from Google Play to determine whether Lifetime Pro is owned and to acknowledge the purchase. In the current client-only design, the purchase token is not sent to a developer-operated server. A last-known entitlement status is stored locally so a verified lifetime purchase can continue during temporary offline periods; ownership is refreshed when Google Play is available.

8. Analytics, advertising, and developer servers

The release described by this policy is intended not to include advertising, analytics, account, cloud-storage, or developer-operated document-processing services. Before each public release, the final dependency graph and merged Android manifest should be re-audited so this policy stays consistent with actual behavior.

9. Data deletion

In DocuScan, open Settings → Privacy & data to erase app-managed document data. This can remove local document records, OCR text, Study Mode data, internal PDFs, thumbnails, tool outputs, cache, and local crash reports. Where supported, the app can also attempt to delete public copies that it previously tracked.

Copies you sent to another app, manually copied files, or historical public files that were never tracked may need to be deleted separately using Android or the receiving app.

10. Children and target audience

DocuScan is designed as a productivity and study utility for teenagers and adults. It is not specifically designed for children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when DocuScan's features or data practices change. The "Last updated" date will be revised when the policy changes.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on DocuScan's Google Play listing or the public project support channel maintained by the developer.
