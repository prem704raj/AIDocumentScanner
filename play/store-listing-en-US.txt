APP NAME
DocuScan: PDF Scanner

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Scan documents, edit PDFs, search OCR text, and organize study notes.

FULL DESCRIPTION

Scan documents, clean up pages, work with PDFs, and find your study material faster with DocuScan.

DocuScan combines a multi-page document scanner, practical PDF tools, on-device OCR search, and student-focused organization in one Android app.

SCAN DOCUMENTS
• Capture one or many pages in a scan session
• Automatic edge detection with confidence-based fallback
• Manual four-corner crop when you need precise control
• Rotate, reorder, duplicate, remove, and retake pages
• Document, grayscale, black-and-white, and color-enhancement filters

CREATE AND MANAGE PDFs
• Turn scanned pages and imported images into PDF documents
• Merge PDFs
• Split pages and ranges
• Remove, reorder, and rotate PDF pages
• Add watermarks
• Protect supported PDFs with a password
• Use safe PDF optimization without fake size guarantees

Some advanced PDF editing tools—Merge, Split, editing pages inside existing PDFs, Watermark, and Password protection—can be unlocked with an optional one-time Lifetime Pro purchase. Scanning, Study Mode, OCR/search, PDF creation/viewing/sharing, Images to PDF, PDF to images, Rename, and safe Optimize remain free.

SEARCH INSIDE DOCUMENTS
• Run bundled OCR locally on supported document pages
• Save OCR text for faster searches after restart
• Search document names and extracted text
• Jump directly to the page containing a match
• Copy or share extracted text when you choose

BUILT FOR STUDENTS
Study Mode helps organize scans around real coursework:
• Create subject folders such as DBMS, DSA, Mathematics, or Physics
• Use presets for Notes, Assignments, Lectures, Whiteboards, Books, and Question Papers
• Apply preset-aware page enhancement
• Generate useful subject-based filenames
• Keep study PDFs grouped by subject

PRIVACY CONTROLS
Core scanning, PDF tools, and bundled OCR are designed to run on your device. No DocuScan account is required for core features.

The Privacy & data screen explains camera access, local storage, OCR data, crash reports, public exports, sharing boundaries, and deletion controls. When you explicitly share or export a file, that copy can leave DocuScan's app-private storage.

DocuScan is designed for students and anyone who needs a focused document scanner and PDF workspace without turning every scan into a cloud workflow.
